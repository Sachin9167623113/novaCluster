Who we are

We are creative architecture Studio

Working alongside architects, developers, and contractors, we deliver integrated architectural and interior solutions built around precision, performance, and innovation. Through facade engineering and BIM-driven workflows, we turn complex design ideas into coordinated, efficient, and build-ready outcomes.

Lucknow International Airport

The architectural development of Lucknow International Airport involved several distinctive façade and canopy elements that required precise digital modeling and detailed technical coordination. The project presented the challenge of converting sophisticated architectural forms into accurate and practical BIM solutions while preserving the intended visual character of the design.

Adani Navi Mumbai International Airport

Complex Column Facade – BIM Development Scope of Work.

  • BIM development of sculptural, façade-integrated structural columns.
  • Precise modeling of complex geometries, curves, and critical junctions.
  • Seamless coordination of column geometry with roof structures and interior systems.
